# ACTION ENGINE - Weekly Bulk Actions

## How It Works

Every Monday morning, I generate:
1. **Debtor Chase List** - Who to follow up for AR
2. **Creditor Delay List** - Who needs a delay message
3. **Compliance Actions** - Super/BAS/Tax due

You review and click **Y** to approve each action.

---

## DEBTOR FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

### Stage 1: Friendly Reminder (Day 0)
```
Subject: Payment Reminder - Invoice [NUMBER] 

Hi [NAME],

I hope this finds you well. I'm following up on Invoice [NUMBER] for $[AMOUNT], which was due on [DATE].

Could you please confirm when we can expect payment? If there are any issues with the invoice, please let me know and I'll address them right away.

Kind regards,
[SENDER]
Mint Invest Group
```

### Stage 2: Follow-Up (Day 7)
```
Subject: Second Notice - Invoice [NUMBER] Overdue

Hi [NAME],

I wanted to follow up on my previous email regarding Invoice [NUMBER] for $[AMOUNT], now [X] days overdue.

Please advise on the expected payment date at your earliest convenience.

If you'd prefer to discuss by phone, I'm available at [PHONE].

Kind regards,
[SENDER]
```

### Stage 3: Escalation (Day 14)
```
Subject: Urgent: Invoice [NUMBER] - Payment Required

Hi [NAME],

Invoice [NUMBER] for $[AMOUNT] is now significantly overdue. 

We need to resolve this matter urgently. Please contact me today to discuss payment arrangements.

If I don't hear from you by [DATE], we will need to consider further action.

Regards,
[SENDER]
```

### Stage 4: Phone Call Script
- Call contact
- Reference invoice number and amount
- Ask for specific payment date
- Offer payment plan if needed
- Document outcome

## CREDITOR DELAY TEMPLATES

### Template A: Cash Flow Delay (Low Risk)
```
Subject: Payment Update - Invoice [NUMBER]

Hi [NAME],

I wanted to reach out regarding Invoice [NUMBER] for $[AMOUNT].

Due to timing of our client payments, we're experiencing a temporary cash flow delay. We expect to settle this invoice by [DATE] - approximately [X] weeks from now.

We value our relationship with [COMPANY] and want to keep you informed. Please let me know if this timing works, or if you'd like to discuss alternative arrangements.

Kind regards,
Zaver Sima
Mint Invest Group
```

### Template B: Partial Payment Offer (Medium Risk)
```
Subject: Payment Proposal - Invoice [NUMBER]

Hi [NAME],

Regarding Invoice [NUMBER] for $[AMOUNT]:

We'd like to propose a payment arrangement:
- $[PARTIAL] paid immediately
- Remaining $[BALANCE] paid by [DATE]

This reflects our commitment to honoring our obligations while managing current cash flow constraints.

Please confirm if this arrangement is acceptable.

Kind regards,
Zaver Sima
```

### Template C: Dispute Resolution (If Applicable)
```
Subject: Invoice [NUMBER] - Clarification Required

Hi [NAME],

Before we proceed with payment on Invoice [NUMBER], we need to clarify:

[SPECIFIC ISSUE - e.g., "The scope of work delivered did not match our agreement..."]

We'd like to resolve this amicably. Could we schedule a call to discuss?

Kind regards,
Zaver Sima
```
